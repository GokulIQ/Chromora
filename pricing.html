Understanding Estimates

What Goes Into Your Painting Estimate?

Size of the Area

Larger surfaces generally require more materials and labor.

Surface Condition

Repairs and preparation can significantly affect the scope.

Paint & Finish Selection

Different coating systems have different material costs.

Number of Coats

Darker color changes or porous surfaces require more coverage.

Accessibility

High walls and difficult elevations require specialized equipment.

Project Complexity

Detailed trim and occupied spaces add preparation time.
