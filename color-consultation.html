EXPERT ADVICE

Choosing the Right Color, Made Easier.

We help you navigate the nuances of light, architecture, and mood to create a palette you'll love.

Natural Lighting

Colors appear cooler in north-facing rooms and warmer in south-facing rooms. Always test samples in your actual lighting.

Warm vs Cool Undertones

Warm tones (reds, yellows, warm greiges) create coziness. Cool tones (blues, greens, cool grays) create serene, expansive feelings.

Paint Finishes

Matte hides imperfections on ceilings. Eggshell is perfect for living rooms. Satin or Semi-gloss provides durability for kitchens, bathrooms, and trim.
