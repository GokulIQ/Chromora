Our Craft

What Happens Before Beautiful Paintwork?

Rigorous Surface Protection

Before opening a single can of paint, we protect your home. Furniture is moved and draped in clean plastic. Hardwood floors are protected with heavy builder's paper, and carpets are shielded with edge-to-edge plastic film.

Comprehensive Filling & Repair

Nail holes, drywall dents, and stress cracks are meticulously patched using high-grade joint compound. On exterior surfaces or woodwork, we use specialized epoxy wood fillers to reconstruct damaged areas.

Sanding for Perfection

Patched areas are pole-sanded perfectly flush with the wall. Glossy trim is deglossed to ensure mechanical adhesion, removing the "nibs" and imperfections from decades of previous paint layers.

Spot & Full Priming

Bare wood, new drywall patches, and heavily stained areas receive targeted primers. This blocks stains from bleeding through and prevents "flashing"—where patched areas look dull under the final finish.

The Final Application

Only after exhaustive preparation do we apply the final coats. Using precise cut-in techniques and premium applicators, we lay down two generous coats of paint for rich, uniform coverage.

Thoughtful Answers for Your Next Home Refresh.

Never paint tiny squares directly on the wall. Paint large pieces of poster board and move them around the room over several days to observe how they react to morning, afternoon, and artificial evening light.

While flat white is standard and safe, consider "color drenching" (painting walls, trim, and ceiling the same color) for a cozy, modern look, or painting the ceiling a soft contrasting tint to draw the eye up.

Professional painters typically work top-down: Ceilings first, followed by walls, and finally the baseboards and trim. This prevents drips from ruining freshly finished lower surfaces.

If you are painting over a similar sheen and color, high-quality paint-and-primer-in-one is usually sufficient. However, if you are painting over gloss, covering a dark color, or painting over patched drywall, a dedicated primer is absolutely necessary.
